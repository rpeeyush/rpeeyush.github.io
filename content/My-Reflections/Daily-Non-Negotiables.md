# My Daily Non-Negotiables

These are the basics I return to every day. Make room for each one, even when the day gets busy.

## Daily checklist

- [ ] Breathing exercises or pranayama
- [ ] Daily journaling
- [ ] Light exercise
- [ ] Prioritize sleep and get adequate rest
- [ ] Naam Jaap
- [ ] Review my To Do list daily

## A moment to reflect

- What did I follow through on today?
- What got in the way?
- What is one small adjustment I can make tomorrow?

Consistency over intensity. Begin again each day.
